Bridges

Historic Railway Architecture

Nine Arches Bridge

The Nine Arches Bridge (Ahas Namaye Palama) is a stone viaduct located between Ella and Demodara stations on the Kandy–Badulla railway line. The bridge stands approximately 24 metres high and 91 metres long, featuring nine graceful arches built entirely from stone, brick, and cement — reportedly without any steel.

Constructed during the British colonial period in the early 20th century, it is now one of Sri Lanka's most iconic landmarks. The bridge is particularly striking when a train crosses it, set against the backdrop of dense tropical vegetation and mountain views.

Category: Heritage / Landmark
Travel note: The bridge is accessible via a short walk from Ella town. Arrive early in the morning or late afternoon for fewer crowds and better light. Check local train timetables to time your visit with a crossing.

Mountains

Peaks & Viewpoints

Mountain

Little Adam's Peak

Named for its resemblance to the sacred Adam's Peak (Sri Pada), Little Adam's Peak is one of Ella's most accessible hikes. The trail begins near the 98 Acres Resort and winds through tea plantations before reaching a grassy ridge with expansive views.

Category: Hiking / Viewpoint
Travel note: The hike is suitable for most fitness levels and takes around 45 minutes to an hour one way. Sunrise and late afternoon offer the best conditions.

Mountain

Ella Rock

Ella Rock is a more demanding hike that rewards visitors with dramatic cliff-edge panoramas. The trail typically begins near the railway line and passes through tea estates and patches of forest before climbing steeply to the summit.

Category: Hiking / Viewpoint
Travel note: The trail can be unclear in places — consider hiring a local guide, especially for early morning hikes. Allow 3–4 hours for the round trip. Carry water and sun protection.

Waterfalls

Cascades & Natural Pools

Ravana Falls

Ravana Falls (Ravana Ella) is one of the widest waterfalls in Sri Lanka. Located along the Ella–Wellawaya road, it cascades approximately 25 metres over a broad rock face. The waterfall is named after King Ravana from the Ramayana epic and is associated with local legends.

The falls are easily accessible from the roadside, and during the wetter months the water flow is particularly impressive. A natural pool at the base is popular for cooling off.

Category: Waterfall / Natural
Travel note: The falls are most impressive during and after the monsoon season. The rocks can be slippery — exercise caution near the water.

Culture & Nature

Heritage & Highland Tea

Heritage

Ravana Cave

Ravana Cave is a natural rock formation located a short distance from Ella town. Associated with the legend of King Ravana, the cave is set into a cliff face and is surrounded by forest. Inside, the cave extends several metres and is home to bats and interesting rock formations.

Category: Heritage / Natural
Travel note: The cave is reached via a short but steep climb. A torch or phone light is useful inside. Combine with a visit to nearby Ravana Falls.

Nature

Tea Plantations

Ella is surrounded by some of Sri Lanka's most photogenic tea estates. The region produces Ceylon tea, one of the country's most important exports. Many estates welcome visitors for guided tours that explain the plucking, withering, rolling, and drying process.

Category: Agriculture / Culture
Travel note: Several tea factories in the Ella area offer tours and tastings. The terraced hillsides are especially beautiful in the morning light.
